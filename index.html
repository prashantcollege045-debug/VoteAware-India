1

Who Can Vote?

To vote in Indian democratic elections, you must satisfy four basic conditions:

  • Indian Citizen: You must hold Indian citizenship.
  • 18+ Years Old: Attained 18 years on or before the qualifying dates (1 Jan, 1 Apr, 1 Jul, 1 Oct). Advance application allowed at 17+.
  • Ordinary Resident: Residing in the constituency where you register.
  • Enrolled in Electoral Roll: Your name must be listed in the current electoral roll of your polling booth.

2

How to Register

Voter registration is 100% free of cost on the official Election Commission of India portal.

  • Form 6: First-time voters and new enrollments.
  • Form 8: Shifting residence, correction of details, or replacement EPIC.
  • Form 6A: Overseas (NRI) Indian citizens.
  • Form 7: Deletion or objection to an entry in the roll.

Applications can be submitted anytime online via voters.eci.gov.in or through the official Voter Helpline App.

3

Electoral Roll

The electoral roll is the official list of registered electors in every polling area.

  • Mandatory Requirement: Having a physical Voter ID card is not enough. You can only vote if your name is actively on the roll.
  • Polling Station Info: The roll provides your designated polling booth, assembly constituency, and serial number.
  • Verify Before Election Day: Check your name online in advance using Electoral Search so there are no surprises on voting day.

4

How Voting Works

On election day, voting follows a smooth, secure standard process at your assigned booth:

  • 1. ID Verification: First polling officer matches your name against the official electoral roll and checks your photo ID.
  • 2. Indelible Ink & Register: Second polling officer marks your left forefinger with indelible ink and records your signature.
  • 3. Secret Voting Compartment: Enter the private booth, press the blue button next to your chosen candidate on the Balloting Unit.
  • 4. Beep & Confirm: A red indicator glows, the VVPAT prints your verification slip, and a long beep confirms your vote.
Official ECI Source Official ECI Source ↗
5

EVM & VVPAT

India's Electronic Voting Machines (EVMs) and Voter Verifiable Paper Audit Trail (VVPAT) ensure transparency:

  • Standalone Devices: EVMs operate on simple independent battery packs. They have no internet, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or wireless connections.
  • 7-Second Paper Verification: When you press the vote button, the VVPAT prints a slip showing the candidate's serial number, name, and symbol behind a glass window.
  • Sealed Audit Box: After 7 seconds, the slip cuts automatically and drops into a locked, tamper-proof compartment used for physical audit verification.
Official ECI Source Official ECI Source ↗
6

Voter Rights & Responsibilities

Informed participation forms the backbone of a vibrant democratic society:

  • Secrecy of Vote: Your ballot choice is confidential by law; no one can compel you to reveal your vote.
  • Accessible Polling: Priority queues, ramps, wheelchairs, and braille voter slips for senior citizens and persons with disabilities.
  • Know Candidates: Right to view affidavits detailing educational, financial, and criminal history.
  • Civic Responsibility: Verify enrollment early, carry valid ID, follow booth rules, and reject unverified rumors.

Fact Check & Clarifications

Myth vs Fact

6 essential verified facts addressing common electoral misconceptions.

Myth
Having a Voter ID automatically means I can vote.
Fact
Your name must appear in the active electoral roll of your polling booth on election day.
Myth
EVMs are connected to Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or the internet.
Fact
EVMs are completely standalone battery-operated devices with zero network connectivity.
Myth
VVPAT paper slips can be taken home by the voter.
Fact
The slip is visible for 7 seconds through a transparent window before automatically dropping into a sealed audit box.
Myth
Online voter registration requires paying a fee.
Fact
Voter registration on the official ECI portal (voters.eci.gov.in) is completely free of charge.
Myth
Only people living in their native hometown can register.
Fact
You can register wherever you are ordinarily resident for study or work, but you can only be enrolled in one place.
Myth
Third-party private websites are required for voter registration.
Fact
Never share personal credentials with private websites. Use the official ECI portal voters.eci.gov.in directly.
